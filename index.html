Community Relocation for Conservation

Why Relocation?

In some regions, moving human communities away from critical habitats helps reduce pressure on ecosystems and gives wildlife room to recover.

Success Stories

From mountain reserves to coastal wetlands, collaborative relocation programs have protected endangered species while offering communities sustainable alternatives.

Get Involved

Support conservation organizations, learn about local relocation initiatives, and share the importance of balancing human needs with wildlife protection.
